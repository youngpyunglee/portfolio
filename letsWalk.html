REFLECTION

In addressing UX/UI design challenges, a significant decision was whether to include seniors in my target demographic. The substantial market potential in the silver healthcare industry, especially in the Korean market, played a pivotal role in this decision. With the government funding healthcare business projects and the success of services like 'Let's Walk,' I decided to cater to both the MZ generation (ages 10 - 20) and seniors (age 65 and above).

This diverse demographic posed a unique challenge in UX/UI design. Seniors often have limited tech skills and struggle with complex navigation, whereas the younger generation prefers simple, clean interfaces. My goal was to balance these needs by creating a user interface that is both straightforward and informative, catering to the sensibilities of both age groups.

While I am confident that my design approach effectively addresses the needs of these diverse users, I recognize the importance of user testing. Gathering feedback from actual users is essential to refine the UX/UI further, ensuring that it not only meets but exceeds the expectations of varied user base.
