REFLECTION

When envisioning this app, my initial concept was a bill-splitting system that would eliminate the need for users to calculate expenses themselves in offline scenarios, such as dining at restaurants. My idea was to provide users with a real-time overview of what they owed through the app, seamlessly linked to the restaurant's POS system, allowing them to make direct payments to the establishment. However, real-world constraints, such as the necessity for agreements between users and store owners, made this system impractical.

As a result, my goal shifted towards offering users more versatile control over their bill-splitting needs, allowing them to divide expenses according to their preferences and convenience. One challenge I encountered was ensuring that all participants in the bill-splitting process used the app, which also required scanning paper receipts for smooth transactions. To enhance user engagement and efficiency, I explored the possibility of seamlessly integrating this app with the store owner's POS system in real-time, enabling automatic receipt retrieval and thus enabling users to engage more effectively in bill splitting.
