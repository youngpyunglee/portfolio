My Experience

Participating in the Kukmin Naeil Baeum Card Training program at ICA Ilsan Computer Arts Academy was a transformative six-month journey for me. Sponsored by the government (Human Resource Department), this course deeply immersed me in the world of smart device content and service design. I delved into user experience analysis, honing my skills in information, UI, and screen design. The hands-on training with tools like Photoshop, Illustrator, and front-end coding not only broadened my technical capabilities but also sharpened my eye for optimizing digital services across various platforms. This program was more than an educational experience; it was a foundational step in my journey, shaping the way I approach app and web design and allowing me to translate my learnings into innovative, user-centric applications.
